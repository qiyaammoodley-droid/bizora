# Bizora — Business Overview

> **Working project name only.** Preliminary web research found existing businesses and software products using “Bizora”, including South African business-related services. Do not treat this name as cleared for commercial use. Complete CIPC, domain, company-name and trademark checks before public launch or investment activity.

**One-line pitch:** A configurable business operations platform that helps service-based businesses manage customers, appointments, payments, staff and service records from one secure workspace.

## The problem

Small and growing service businesses often coordinate daily work across spreadsheets, messaging apps, paper records, calendars and separate payment records. This fragmentation makes it harder to find customer information, coordinate schedules, follow up on unpaid invoices, understand business performance and maintain consistent service histories.

The problem is not identical in every industry, but recurring operational needs are shared: businesses serve customers, deliver services, coordinate people and schedules, collect payments and keep records.

## Target customer

Initial focus: small and medium-sized service businesses in South Africa that manage recurring customer relationships or scheduled services.

Potential segments:
- Children's development and activity providers
- Sports academies and coaching businesses
- Tutoring and training centres
- Dance and music schools
- Beauty and wellness providers
- Independent professional service providers

**Buyer:** business owner or authorised manager.  
**Daily users:** administrators, managers and employees/service providers.  
**Potential external users later:** customers, parents or guardians through a limited customer portal.

KidzAbility is a potential discovery and pilot partner, not a confirmed customer. Its workflows must be validated with the owner before being treated as requirements.

## The solution

Bizora is planned as a multi-tenant SaaS product. Each subscribing organisation has a separate workspace with organisation-scoped customers, staff, appointments, invoices, payments and business records. Shared core modules can be configured for different service industries without building a separate application for each one.

## Expected impact and measurement

The product aims to reduce fragmented administration and improve operational visibility. Pilot impact will be measured rather than assumed.

| Measure | How it will be evaluated |
|---|---|
| Administrative time | Compare time spent on selected tasks before and during the pilot |
| Payment visibility | Reconcile invoices, recorded payments and outstanding balances |
| Scheduling reliability | Track missed, cancelled and conflicting appointments |
| Record completeness | Measure whether required customer/service records are maintained |
| Adoption | Weekly active staff and businesses |
| Satisfaction | Short interviews and task-based feedback |

No impact results are claimed before a real pilot.

## Business model

Proposed SaaS subscription, billed monthly or annually to the business rather than its customers. Early pricing hypotheses for validation:

| Plan | Indicative monthly price | Intended customer |
|---|---:|---|
| Starter | R199 | Independent provider |
| Growth | R399 | Small team |
| Professional | R699 | Established business |
| Enterprise | Custom | Multiple branches or larger organisations |

These are hypotheses, not validated market prices. Potential later add-ons include additional locations, advanced reporting, custom branding and integrations.

## Go-to-market

1. Conduct discovery interviews with local service-business owners.
2. Use one willing business as a pilot partner and map its current workflow.
3. Offer guided onboarding and a time-limited pilot.
4. Demonstrate the product to businesses in adjacent segments such as academies, tutoring centres and studios.
5. Build referral relationships with business networks and local service providers.
6. Use case studies and measurable pilot outcomes to support conversion.

The first 100 customers are a growth target, not an existing result. Acquisition channels and conversion assumptions must be tested.

## Competitors and alternatives

The product competes with a mix of manual tools, general-purpose CRM platforms and industry-specific software.

Examples to investigate:
- Spreadsheets, paper files, WhatsApp and calendar tools
- HubSpot and other general CRM products
- Fresha and Booksy for appointment-led beauty businesses
- Mindbody for fitness and wellness businesses

These products serve different needs and are not all direct substitutes for every target segment. A competitor matrix should be completed using current product features, pricing and target markets before making superiority claims.

## Why this approach

The project starts from a practical operational scenario observed through KidzAbility, while designing reusable capabilities that may serve adjacent service industries. The founder's exposure to logistics, customer support, stock receiving and data capturing provides useful context for operational workflows. The product will be validated with users rather than assuming one business represents an entire market.

## Risks and constraints

- Broad scope can produce a generic, overcomplicated product. The MVP must remain focused.
- Multi-tenant data isolation is a critical security requirement.
- Children's information may be sensitive. Collect the minimum necessary data, restrict access, obtain appropriate consent and assess POPIA obligations before processing real children's data.
- A short capstone deadline limits production readiness. The live MVP must be described honestly.
- The working name is not cleared for commercial use.

## MVP scope

1. Authentication and organisation workspace
2. Owner and employee roles
3. Customer management
4. Appointment/session scheduling and attendance
5. Invoices and manually recorded payments
6. Basic customer/service records and operational summaries

Online payment processing, payroll, full accounting, inventory, marketing automation and advanced customer portals are out of scope for the initial MVP.
