# Portfolio project entry for Bizora

Use this content when adding Bizora to the projects section of your portfolio.

**Project name:** Bizora

**Short description:** A multi-tenant business operations workspace for service-based businesses, bringing customer management, appointment scheduling, invoices and manually recorded payments into one application.

**Technology:** React, TypeScript, Vite, Tailwind CSS, Supabase Auth, PostgreSQL, Row Level Security, Vitest, Vercel.

**Live demo:** https://bizora-qiyaam.vercel.app

**Source code:** https://github.com/qiyaammoodley-droid/bizora

**Feature highlights:**
- Secure sign-in and organisation workspace setup
- Live dashboard with customer, booking and invoice summaries
- Customer management
- Appointment scheduling and status management
- Invoice creation and partial/manual payment recording
- Organisation-scoped database access with Supabase RLS

**Important accuracy note:** Do not list employee invitations, advanced analytics, payment gateway processing or other unfinished sidebar modules as completed features.
